# Data sources and availability

The completed numerical matrices and supplementary Tables S1–S6 are available
directly in [supporting information](00_supporting_information/README.md).
The compact, checksummed bundle contains the audited human/bat transcript means,
fixed human gene-representative means, annotation records and six NNC preference
tables. No rerun or institutional HPC account is required. It can accompany the
paper as supplementary data; no DOI-bearing deposit is claimed.

Keep new working outputs, raw references and full checkpoints in the project
directories described by [the reproduction guide](MANUSCRIPT_REPRODUCTION.md),
outside the Git checkout.

## References and prepared inputs

[Human preparation](01_data_downloading_and_preprocessing/README.md) defines the CDS-plus sequence and historical rejection rules. [Bat preparation](06_Bat_genome/author_hpc/PREPARATION.md) uses the frozen transcript populations and source snapshots rather than substituting a newer annotation. Download manifests distinguish configured/preserved hashes from independently supplied publisher checksums.

The experimental source workbook is included with project-owner authorization. Conversion uses the 16 motif-average values per enzyme, normalized as per-opportunity weights; exact historical probability CSVs were not recovered. Raw assay reads and the upstream read-processing workflow are not included.

The archived S11 table contains historical simulation estimates, not fresh
corrected-package trajectories. Old top-level workbooks retain their bytes and
legacy names. Current manuscript numbering and regenerated outputs are clearly
separated in `00_supporting_information/paper_tables/`. Bat transcript matrices
are not presented as orthologue-standardized gene matrices.

## COSMIC observation access

Use the [download instructions](01_data_downloading_and_preprocessing/COSMIC_VARIANTS.md), then [breast selection and coordinate mapping](01_data_downloading_and_preprocessing/COSMIC_BREAST_VARIANTS.md). Registration/account access and the provider's terms apply. No account credentials, signed download URLs, raw COSMIC mutation tables or processed restricted observation tables are included here. v101 is the declared reconstruction release; its identity with the original historical release is not established.

## Preserve a campaign

Retain raw-file hashes, input manifests, full audits and final artwork with each
calculation. Record both commits and the resolved environment. The published
numerical bundle has `data_manifest.json`, `SHA256SUMS` and a standard-library
verifier. It is not a full trajectory archive. Add an archive DOI only when that
archive has actually been deposited.
